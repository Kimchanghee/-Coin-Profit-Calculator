Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-13. ProfitCalc is a browser-based futures scenario calculator. It has no user accounts and does not ask for exchange credentials, API keys or personal identifiers.

Calculator inputs

Entry price, target price, investment, leverage, fee and payback values are processed locally in your browser to produce the result. They are not sent to ProfitCalc for the calculation.

Analytics

The site loads Google Analytics (Google Tag Manager measurement script) to count page visits and referral-link clicks. Google may set cookies or similar identifiers and receive your IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy. You can block the script with your browser settings or an extension.

Referral links

When a referral or partner link is shown, clicking it takes you to a third-party exchange site that has its own privacy policy. A click event may be recorded in analytics. Referral relationships may generate compensation and are not endorsements.

Language preference

The selected interface language may be stored in your browser so the page opens in the same language next time.

Changes

This page is updated when data handling changes; the update date is shown above.
