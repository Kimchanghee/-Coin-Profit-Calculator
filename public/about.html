About ProfitCalc

ProfitCalc is an informational scenario calculator, not a broker, exchange or financial adviser. No live price feed or account connection is required. No forecast, return or rebate is guaranteed.

This is a linear USD-denominated model with fixed units and exact entry and exit prices, not an exchange settlement engine. Funding, slippage, taxes, inverse contracts, maintenance margin and exchange-specific rules are not modeled. Final equity may be negative; no exchange loss cap is applied. Payback is a hypothetical reduction of both trading fees, not a guaranteed rebate. This is information, not financial advice.

The interface supports English, Korean, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, German, French, Russian, Portuguese and Hindi. These reference guides are in English.
