Trading Fee Impact

Crypto futures profit should be checked after both entry and exit fees. With leverage, fees are usually charged on position value, not just margin.

Fee formula

Entry fee = position value * fee rate

Exit fee = exit value * fee rate

Net PnL = gross PnL - entry fee - exit fee + referral payback

Answer summary

Always compare gross PnL and net PnL. A profitable trade can become smaller after fees, especially at high leverage or with frequent entries and exits.
