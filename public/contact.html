Contact

ProfitCalc is a free futures scenario calculator maintained by a small team. For calculator errors, corrections or general questions, open an issue in the public project repository:

github.com/Kimchanghee/-Coin-Profit-Calculator/issues

What to include

The inputs you used (entry price, target price, leverage, fees), the result you expected and the result you saw. Do not post API keys, exchange credentials or personal information.

What we cannot do

We do not give trading or investment advice, and we cannot recover funds or help with exchange accounts. ProfitCalc results are scenario estimates only.

Corrections policy

Confirmed calculation errors are fixed and the update date on the affected page is changed. See the methodology for the formulas used.
